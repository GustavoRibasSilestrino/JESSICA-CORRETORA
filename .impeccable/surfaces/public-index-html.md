# Surface brief: home (public/index.html)

Scope: the single-page site of Jessica Albuquerque. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a buyer or owner of a high-end property in Sorocaba, usually on a phone, deciding whether Jessica is someone to trust with a large, private transaction. Action: start a private WhatsApp conversation. Proof available: none beyond the consultative method itself; no numbers, testimonials or listings are invented. Property cards are illustrative profiles, labelled as such.

Confirmed answers: Sorocaba; WhatsApp plus a short form that composes the message (number is a placeholder until supplied); Unsplash stock architecture for the inner sections, hotlinked, to be replaced. User decisions during the build: the entrance uses the user's own mansion image; Poppins is the only typeface; after a first version the user asked for a more beautiful, less AI-looking site built with ui-ux-pro-max, which replaced the navy and gold world, the arch frames and the centred hero.

Unresolved: WhatsApp number, CRECI, Instagram, e-mail, real photos, real listings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: You do not browse a catalogue, you arrive at a house. The scroll walks the visitor toward a mansion at dusk and through its front door, and lands them inside a lit living room; the site is what is inside. Refuses the category default of a static photo hero with a search bar and a grid of listings.

OWN-WORLD: The house in the user's photograph sets every rule. Coffee-black of its door and fascias (#1C1917), warm white (#FAFAF9), the pale stone of its walls (#F1EEE9), hairlines (#D6D3D1), and one accent, brass: #955A06 on light, #E4B869 (the lit windows) on dark. Brass means light or action only. Straight lines and square corners like the architecture: no radius, no shadows, no gradients as decoration. Poppins only (user-pinned): weight 300 for display with tight tracking, 400 for text, 500 uppercase at wide tracking for labels. Photography carries the page; layouts sit on a 12-column grid and are deliberately asymmetric.

STORY: Understands that this is one named broker, not an agency; believes she listens before she shows; taps the WhatsApp action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The user's mansion photograph, full width, door pinned at 62% of the viewport height, with authored sky above on tall screens. The name set large and light in white over the sky, the role line under it, "Pular introdução" top right, "Role para entrar" at the bottom. Scrolling scales the photograph from the door, captions pass, the door leaf (a crop of the same photograph) swings on its hinge, warm light fills the frame, and the eye adjusts to a full-bleed living room with the headline bottom-left, the lede and the primary WhatsApp button.

FORM: User-pinned direction (scroll dolly into a mansion, the site appears once inside); a pinned direction beats the roll, so no ordered list position applies. seed:7cb13ed5fab8b9d8. Signature interaction: the scroll-driven approach and the door opening. Motion grammar: one authored moment, then stillness; only photographs unveil as they enter view. Structure after the entrance: statement and principles, an index of three services where each row is a WhatsApp link, four numbered steps on coffee-black, an offset three-image gallery, and a contact section that brings the mansion back behind the form.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
