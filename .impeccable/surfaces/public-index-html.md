# Surface brief: home (public/index.html)

Scope: the single-page site of Jessica Albuquerque. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: a buyer or owner of a high-end property in Sorocaba, usually on a phone, deciding whether Jessica is someone to trust with a large, private transaction. Action: start a private WhatsApp conversation. Proof available: none beyond the consultative method itself; no numbers, testimonials or listings are invented. Property cards are illustrative profiles, labelled as such.

Confirmed answers: Sorocaba; WhatsApp plus a short form that composes the message (number is a placeholder until supplied); Unsplash stock architecture for the inner sections, hotlinked, to be replaced. User decisions during the build: the entrance uses the user's own mansion image; Poppins is the only typeface.

Unresolved: WhatsApp number, CRECI, Instagram, e-mail, real photos, real listings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: You do not browse a catalogue, you arrive at a house. The scroll walks the visitor toward a mansion at dusk and through its front door; the site is what is inside. Refuses the category default of a static photo hero with a search bar and a grid of listings.

OWN-WORLD: Blue hour. Night indigo (#0A0B2A to #15184A), the periwinkle and rose sky of the user's photograph, cool marble (#EFF1F6) inside, and one warm material: window gold (#F3B757), the light that is on. Gold only ever means light or action. The round-headed doorway is the recurring shape: hero outline, image frames, the contact form. Poppins throughout (user-pinned), weight 500 for display with tight tracking, 300 for the entrance captions.

STORY: Understands that this is one named broker, not an agency; believes she listens before she shows; taps the WhatsApp action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The user's mansion photograph, full width, door pinned at 62% of the viewport height, with authored sky above and paving below on tall screens. The name set large in white over the sky, the role line under it, "Pular introdução" top right, "Role para entrar" at the bottom. Scrolling scales the photograph from the door, captions pass, the door leaf (a crop of the same photograph) swings on its hinge, warm light fills the frame and resolves into the hero: headline, lede and the primary WhatsApp button.

FORM: User-pinned direction (scroll dolly into a mansion, the site appears once inside); a pinned direction beats the roll, so no ordered list position applies. seed:7cb13ed5fab8b9d8. Signature interaction: the scroll-driven approach and the door opening. Motion grammar: one authored moment, then quiet reveals; arched windows that light as each method step enters view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
